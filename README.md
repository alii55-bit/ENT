About ENT

ENT is a global scientific framework for the study of:

- structural stability
- human exhaustion
- coordinated development
- civilization sustainability
- energy responsibility
- and integrated structural systems

within the existing world.

ENT examines human beings, biological structures, cellular systems, and structural patterns of the surrounding world as interconnected components of a living coordinated system.

Civilization Transition

ENT studies the ongoing structural transition of human civilization toward greater coordination, lower exhaustion, and sustainable development.

The objective is not domination or ideological control, but understanding and improving structural stability within the existing world.
